Proven Commons publishes its original code and documents under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The org owner authorised this choice on 2026-10-28, as recorded on [mission issue 9](https://github.com/Proven-Commons/mission/issues/9). The same license covers the founding brief, playbook, review records and original website material in this repository.

Each contributor retains copyright in their own work. Contributing does not transfer copyright to Foundation-0 or to another contributor. Unless a contributor says otherwise, a contribution comes in under the same license (Apache-2.0, section 5). The standard license appendix is an example notice, not a claim that the org owns someone else's work.

Existing notices and the licenses of outside material remain in effect. A repository's license does not relicense a standard, test corpus, dependency, font or other material copied from elsewhere. Keep the source and license notices with such material.

Every library repository includes the unmodified Apache-2.0 text in `LICENSE`. A Lake package also sets `license = "Apache-2.0"` in `lakefile.toml`. New libraries start with the licensed template in `bar/template`.

The license grants reuse rights. It does not mean that a library meets the [verified bar](playbook/verified-bar.md).
